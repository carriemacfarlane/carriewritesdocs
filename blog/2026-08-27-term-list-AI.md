I’ve cleared some space in my schedule to work on content strategy. There’s lots to do, so I’m going to try to enlist AI. I started a subscription to Claude Pro, and I plan to tell my department how it goes. Can Claude Pro help with content strategy?

Inspired by Tara English-Sweeney, who used AI for translation-type work, and by Tom Johnson, who used AI to build a glossary, the first area I'm exploring is terminology management.

I have a 3 year-old glossary of user interface elements. It’s become a long and unwieldy Google doc. I've now spent several afternoons iterating with Claude. After taking a few regrettable trips down rabbit holes, I've built a proof-of-concept spreadsheet.  

A key feature of the spreadsheet is identifiers. Every term has a unique and permanent ID. This should make it easier for me to manage terminology changes over time. The ID, along with *parent* and *navigation* columns, also should allow writers to confirm they're using current names.

I like this new format, and despite my librarian superpowers, I probably would have needed more than several afternoons to come up with it on my own. My next step is to test the spreadsheet with a larger term set. If everything checks out, I’ll share it with the team for feedback.
