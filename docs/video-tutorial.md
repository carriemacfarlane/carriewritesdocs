# Video Tutorial

This video was part of an online assignment that preceded the PsycINFO training sessions I led when I was a librarian. The students watched the video and experimented with the database, then asked questions when they met with me. Because they had already tried using the database on their own, they came to the training with a clear sense of what they needed to learn.
