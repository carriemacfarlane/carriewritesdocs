# Quick Reference

As a librarian, I taught college students how to use a research database called PsycINFO. Before the students met with me, I had them complete an online exercise that introduced the tools they'd need for their first research assignment. When the students arrived in the classroom, this quick reference guide reminded them of what they learned in the online exercise.
